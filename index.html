    Each day has its own deck, numbered by the day of the year. Your score is kept beside any you have played. Tap a day to play it.

      How to play

      1. You get ten acronyms, each with what it supposedly stands for. You are judging the card: does it really stand for that?
      2. Some are real. The rest were made up — sometimes a real acronym under a meaning that is not its own, sometimes an acronym nobody has ever issued. How many of each changes every game, so counting won't help you.
      3. Decide on each one. One point for every one you get right.

      One deck a day. Everyone gets the same ten, drawn from all three levels. Your first score on a day is the one that is kept, and playing every day builds a streak. Random puzzles are unlimited, at the level you choose, and go towards your personal best instead.
